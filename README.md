# Super Sales Analysis | SQL • Excel • Power BI

## Project Overview

**Super Sales Analysis** is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing sales performance and generating actionable business insights from a Superstore dataset.

The project uses **PostgreSQL for data analysis, Microsoft Excel for exploratory analysis and reporting, and Power BI for interactive dashboarding**.

The analysis covers sales performance across customers, products, categories, segments, regions, shipping modes, and time periods.

---

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to understand sales performance and answer key business questions such as:

* Which customers generate the highest sales?
* Which products contribute the most revenue?
* Which categories and regions perform best?
* How does sales performance vary by customer segment?
* Which shipping modes generate the highest sales?
* How have sales changed over time?
* What is the year-over-year sales growth?

---

## Dataset

The project uses a Superstore sales dataset containing **9,800 sales records**.

### Key Attributes

* Order and shipping dates
* Shipping mode
* Customer information
* Customer segment
* Geographic information
* Product and category information
* Sales amount

The primary dataset contains **18 business columns**, including:

`Order_ID`, `Order_Date`, `Ship_Date`, `Ship_Mode`, `Customer_ID`, `Customer_Name`, `Segment`, `Country`, `City`, `State`, `Region`, `Product_ID`, `Category`, `Sub_Category`, `Product_Name`, and `Sales`.

## SQL Analysis

The PostgreSQL analysis includes:

### Sales Performance

* Total sales
* Sales by shipping mode
* Sales by customer segment
* Sales by category
* Sales by region

### Customer & Product Analysis

* Top 10 customers by sales
* Top 10 products by sales
* Top 5 states by sales

### Time-Series Analysis

* Monthly sales by year
* Yearly sales
* Year-over-year sales growth

The project also uses SQL window functions such as **`LAG()`** to compare annual sales performance with the previous year.
